Published webinar attendance benchmarks range from 38% to 60% of registrants, depending on whose platform the data comes from. ON24 reports 60%, TwentyThree 58%, Livestorm 51.3% and Demio 38%. The most useful number is your own: measure your registration-to-attendance rate on every webinar and compare each event against it.

This page collects the webinar statistics we consider credible, with a link to each source. We update it when new benchmark reports are published.

## Why the benchmarks disagree

Each number comes from one vendor's own customers, measured its own way:

- **Different audiences.** An enterprise platform's customers run different events, with different promotion budgets, than a platform popular with small teams.
- **Different definitions.** "Registration-to-attendee conversion", "show-up rate" and "live-session attendance" are not always calculated the same way, for example whether on-demand viewers count.
- **Different years.** Demio's figure describes 2022; ON24's describes 2025.

So treat these as a range, not a target. A B2B webinar landing at 40% is not failing, and one at 60% is not guaranteed to stay there.

## Audience size and engagement

- [ON24 reports](https://www.on24.com/blog/key-takeaways-from-the-webinar-benchmarks-report/) an average of 254 attendees per webinar in Q4 2025, up from 219 a year earlier, and an average engagement time of 49 minutes.
- TwentyThree data cited by [Zoom](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/webinar-statistics/) puts the average at 307 sign-ups per webinar.
- BigMarker data cited by [Zoom](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/webinar-statistics/) found high-performing webinars averaged 375 registrants and 214 attendees.

## Timing and reminders

- BigMarker data cited by [Zoom](https://www.zoom.com/en/blog/webinar-statistics/) shows 50-55% response rates for webinars held during business hours, against 25-30% outside them.
- [Banzai's analysis of about 800,000 webinars](https://www.banzai.io/2024-webinar-statistics) run on Demio found that attendance rises when brands use email notifications, regardless of company size.
- [Livestorm reports](https://livestorm.co/blog/boost-webinar-attendance-rate) that browser-based webinar platforms showed a 53% higher attendance rate than traditional, download-based ones.

## How to use these numbers

1. **Calculate your baseline.** Attendance rate = attendees / registrants. Use your last three to five webinars.
2. **Set a relative goal.** Aim to beat your own baseline by a few points per event rather than chasing another platform's average.
3. **Change one thing at a time** (timing, a new reminder channel, a stronger reason to attend live) so you know what moved the number.

**Example:** 300 registrants at a 38% baseline is 114 attendees. Reaching 45% is 135 attendees, 21 more people, from the same promotion effort.

## Key takeaways

- Credible attendance benchmarks sit between 38% and 60% of registrants.

## FAQ

### What is a good webinar attendance rate?
Published benchmarks range from 38% (Demio, 2022) to 60% (ON24, 2025). A good rate is one that beats your own recent average; measure attendees divided by registrants for your last few webinars and aim to improve on that.

### Why do webinar statistics from different sources disagree?
Each vendor reports on its own customers, uses its own definition of attendance and covers a different year, so the numbers are not directly comparable.

### How many people attend the average webinar?
ON24 reports an average of 254 attendees per webinar in Q4 2025. TwentyThree data cited by Zoom puts average sign-ups at 307 per webinar.

### Does the time of a webinar affect attendance?
BigMarker data cited by Zoom shows 50-55% response rates for webinars during business hours versus 25-30% outside them.

### Do reminder emails increase webinar attendance?
Banzai's analysis of about 800,000 webinars on Demio found that attendance increases when brands use email notifications, regardless of company size.
