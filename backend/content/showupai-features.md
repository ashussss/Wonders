ShowUpAI is a webinar attendance tool: you add your webinar, and it writes, schedules and sends an 11-touch reminder sequence across email, LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp/SMS, Circle.so and calendar invites, starting three weeks before the event and ending with follow-ups after it. You approve every message before it goes out. The goal is simple: make more of the people who register actually show up.

## Why a registration is not an attendee

Webinar benchmarks vary a lot depending on whose customers you look at. [ON24 reports a 60% average registration-to-attendee conversion](https://www.on24.com/blog/key-takeaways-from-the-webinar-benchmarks-report/) across its platform in 2025, while [Demio (Banzai) reported a 38% average live attendance rate](https://www.banzai.io/2023-webinar-stats-for-marketers) for its customers in 2022. Either way, a big share of the people who sign up never join.

The gap is rarely about the topic. People register on impulse, then the event date arrives in a week full of meetings, and nothing reminded them why they cared. [Banzai's analysis of about 800,000 webinars found that attendance rises when brands use email notifications](https://www.banzai.io/2024-webinar-statistics). ShowUpAI takes that idea further: more touches, more channels, and messages that give people a reason to come, not just a date.

**Example:** if 300 people register and 38% attend, that's 114 people in the room. Lift attendance to 50% and you get 150, which is 36 extra people hearing your pitch, from the same promotion budget.

## How ShowUpAI works in 4 steps

1. **Add your webinar.** Paste the event page URL and ShowUpAI pulls in the details, or create it manually. If you run a Circle.so community, you can pull in upcoming Circle events too.
2. **Get your send plan.** ShowUpAI builds the full 11-touch sequence around your event date and writes the copy for every channel, with two versions of each message: a polished one and a more casual, urgent one.
3. **Review and approve.** Edit anything, regenerate a message, pick the version you like, and approve. Nothing sends without your approval.
4. **Let it run.** Registrants come in through your ShowUpAI registration page, a CSV import or the Circle webhook. Approved touches go out on schedule, and every send is logged.

## The 11-touch sequence

| # | Touch | When | Default channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Registration confirmation + calendar invite | On registration | Email |
| 2 | Awareness post | 21 days before | Email, LinkedIn page, Facebook |
| 3 | Newsletter / insight | 19 days before | Email, LinkedIn page |
| 4 | Engagement poll | 14 days before | LinkedIn page, Facebook, Instagram, Circle |
| 5 | Case study | 10 days before | Email, LinkedIn page, Facebook |
| 6 | Infographic teaser | 8 days before | Instagram, LinkedIn page, Facebook |
| 7 | Urgency message | 5 days before | Email, WhatsApp |
| 8 | Final warm-up | 1 day before | Email, LinkedIn page, LinkedIn personal |
| 9 | Join link | 1 hour before | Email, WhatsApp, Circle |
| 10 | Thank-you | After the event (attended) | Email |
| 11 | "You missed it" follow-up | 2 days after (no-shows) | Email |

Most tools send a confirmation and one reminder the day before. ShowUpAI starts building interest three weeks out, keeps the event in front of people with value (a poll, a case study, an infographic), and only then moves to urgency and the join link.

## Features

### Multi-channel delivery
- **Email** through your own Brevo, Mailchimp, SendGrid or Buzz.ai account, so messages come from your domain.
- **LinkedIn company page**, **Facebook page** and **Instagram** posts.
- **WhatsApp**, with SMS as a fallback, through Twilio.
- **Circle.so** community posts.
- **Calendar invite (.ics)** attached to the confirmation so the event lands in their calendar from day one.
- **LinkedIn personal** posts are prepared for you to copy and paste. They are never posted automatically.

### AI copy you stay in control of
Every touch is written for your specific webinar, speaker and audience, per channel, in two variants. You can edit, regenerate one message or the whole sequence, and approve in bulk from the **Approval Queue**.

### Content that gives people a reason to come
- **Social images**: a 6-slide carousel, an infographic and a quote card, generated for your webinar.
- **Lead magnets**: a case study, shareable snippets and a one-pager PDF.
- **AI Quick Post**: a one-off LinkedIn or Circle post with an optional poll, when you want an extra push.

### Event-day and after
- **Go live** sends an instant "we're live" message with the join link.
- **Mark attendance** after the event and ShowUpAI sends different follow-ups to attendees and no-shows.
- **ShowUp Score**: a post-event image of your attendance result you can share on LinkedIn.

### Analytics
Attendance rate by channel, community-join attribution for Circle, email analytics and a schedule view of every upcoming send, so you can see which touches actually brought people in.

### Security
Your provider API keys are encrypted at rest and never shown in full in the app.

## FAQ

### Does ShowUpAI replace Zoom or my webinar platform?
No. ShowUpAI works alongside the webinar platform you already use. It handles everything between registration and the event, plus the follow-up, and sends people your existing join link.

### Will ShowUpAI send messages without my approval?
No. Every touch waits in your Approval Queue until you approve it. You can edit or regenerate any message first.

### Which channels does ShowUpAI support?
Email (through Brevo, Mailchimp, SendGrid or Buzz.ai), LinkedIn company pages, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp with SMS fallback, Circle.so and calendar invites. LinkedIn personal posts are prepared for you to paste yourself.
