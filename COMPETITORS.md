# Competitor & SERP research: webinar attendance content

_Researched 24 September 2026. Re-check before writing comparison pages; features and pricing change._

## Who ranks for "increase webinar attendance" style queries

Almost every ranking page is a **webinar platform's own blog** using attendance content as top-of-funnel for its platform:

| Company | What they are | How their attendance content works |
|---|---|---|
| **Livestorm** | Browser-based webinar platform | "15 proven tactics" listicle; cites its own platform stat (51.3% average show-up); pushes automated reminders + browser-based joining |
| **WebinarJam** | Live webinar platform | "12 proven tactics (2026)"; honest angle: your own baseline is the target, count replay viewers too; includes a payback calculator |
| **eWebinar** | Automated/on-demand webinars | Reminder content tied to its built-in sequence (confirmation, 1 day, 1 hour, WhatsApp integration) |
| **EasyWebinar** | Live + evergreen webinars | Very long, opinionated posts with customer case numbers (K21 Academy), G2 quotes, industry-specific show-up ranges |
| **Demio (Banzai)** | Marketer-focused webinar platform | Data-driven attendance-rate posts; annual "webinar stats" pages from its own platform data |
| **ON24** | Enterprise webinar/event platform | Annual Digital Engagement Benchmarks report: the most-cited primary source in the whole niche |
| **Airmeet** | Virtual/hybrid event platform | "Best AI webinar platform to reduce no-shows"; AI-personalised reminder angle |
| **WebinarNinja, Univid, Glue Up** | Webinar / community platforms | Glossary-style and "how to improve attendance rate" posts |
| **Stats aggregators** (AONMeetings, Digital Applied, VoxBooster, CO Consulting, CUFinder) | Content sites | "Webinar statistics 2026" pages compiling ON24/GoTo/Demio/Contrast numbers with source links; these get cited by AI answers |

## What this means for ShowUp.ai

- **No one owns "attendance as its own product".** Competitors are platforms where reminders are one feature. ShowUp.ai's angle is platform-agnostic: works with Zoom, Teams, Livestorm, anything, across email + LinkedIn + WhatsApp + Circle, with human approval.
- **Generic "how to increase attendance" posts are saturated** and written by domains with years of authority. A new domain won't outrank them on head terms quickly.
- **What wins (Google + AI answers):**
  1. **Original data.** ON24, Demio and Livestorm rank and get cited because they publish their *own* numbers. ShowUp.ai should publish aggregate, anonymised attendance data from its own platform as soon as there is enough.
  2. **Source-led statistics pages** with a clear methodology and a link on every number.
  3. **First-hand experience**: named author, real examples, real message copy, what didn't work.
  4. **Specific long-tail angles** big platforms ignore: WhatsApp reminders, Circle/community events, LinkedIn event reminders, edtech/agency use cases, India/APAC time zones.
  5. **Comparison / alternative pages** ("X vs ShowUp.ai", "best tools to reduce webinar no-shows") built from verified fact sheets.
  6. **Off-site mentions**: G2/Capterra listings, LinkedIn posts, Reddit/community answers, guest posts. AI engines weigh how often a brand is mentioned elsewhere, not just its own site.

## Verified facts in use (pseo.py `FACTS`)
- ON24 2026 Digital Engagement Benchmarks: 60% registration-to-attendee conversion, 49-minute average engagement, Q4 2025 average 254 attendees (219 a year earlier).
- Livestorm: 51.3% average show-up rate across industries (its platform).
- Demio/Banzai: 38% average live attendance in 2022, Feb/Mar highest at 41%.

Benchmarks disagree because each reflects one vendor's customers. Always say whose data it is.
